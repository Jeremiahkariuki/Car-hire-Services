Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need at vehicle pickup?

You need your physical valid driver's license (held for at least 1 year), a passport or national government ID, and your RideKari booking reference code.

Is there an extra charge for extra kilometers?

No! All standard rentals include 100% unlimited mileage within the state or national territory.

Can I return the car at a different location?

Yes, one-way rentals are supported between any of our active branch locations and airport hubs without hidden penalties.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

If you entered your flight number in the special requests during booking, our airport desk will track your flight and hold your vehicle until your arrival.
